2 Get the review button

💬 Add a comment

Share this with reviewers:
1. Drag the “💬 Add a comment” button to your bookmarks bar (one time).
2. Open the site you're reviewing
3. Click the 💬 Add a comment bookmark. A panel appears top-right.
4. Enter your email, hit + Add comment, then click any element or text and type your note.
5. Everyone’s comments sync automatically — reply and mark items resolved right on the page.
